Our Story

A Taste Of Italy,
Right at Your Fingertips

Esperienza Como began with one simple goal in mind: to deliver the essence of Italian hospitality right to your table. Our restaurant offers you a fusion of authentic recipes, hand-made ingredients, and a love for fine dining.

From our hand-made pasta to wood-fired pizzas, each meal is prepared with great care, like that in an Italian house.
